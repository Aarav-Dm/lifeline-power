# PRODUCT — LifeLine Power (context for the impeccable skill; full detail in PRD.md)

- **Users:** home-care coordinators, or an adult child acting as one, registering someone on powered medical equipment in a Massachusetts town. Demo: Dana Okonkwo (synthetic) on the phone with a family.
- **Job:** type a town, see only that town's electric company and that company's own medical program, and print a one-page handoff naming the next unfinished step and who owns it.
- **Tone:** calm, clinical, plain English. A work tool, not a landing page. Honest states: blocked, mixed-company and unconfirmed are shown, never guessed.
- **Brand:** medical teal on a pale teal page; Figtree headings, Noto Sans body; one signature element, the "Next step" band (see FRONTEND.md, the design contract).
- **Anti-references:** marketing heroes, stats strips, gradients, glassmorphism, emoji icons, dark mode, anything that implies power is guaranteed or a utility has confirmed enrollment.
- **Principles:** every rule shows its source; missing is "UNVERIFIED", never filled in; keyboard and 44 px targets; WCAG AA contrast; print is plain black on white.
