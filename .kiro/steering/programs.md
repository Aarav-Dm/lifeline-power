# Program records

Load this only while those two files are in play. The company line is `utility_code`. Never use the `utility` category column as the company.

Keep each record separate. Outage notification and shutoff protection are not one card. `ng-paper` is not a National Grid program.

`Holyoke Gas & Electric Department` has no record. Say "No form opened. No checklist." Any other company with no opened form says "No form loaded for this company in this build. No checklist." `National Grid (Nantucket Electric)` is not `National Grid (Massachusetts Electric)`.

Do not copy Wellesley's calendar-year expiry onto another company. Do not write "protected for 12 months". The EJ mean does not choose a language. Completing a checklist does not set Confirmed.
