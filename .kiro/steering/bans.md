# Bans — LifeLine Power

These are refusals, not preferences. If a change would break one, stop and say which ban it breaks.

1. Never merge two programs. Each record keeps its own purpose, signer, renewal, return channel, quotes and limits. Outage notification and shutoff protection are never one card.
2. Never carry an answer, attestation, company pick, program choice or typed county across a town or company change. Clear it. Language is the only field that survives.
3. Never attach a renewal the selected record's source does not state. National Grid Outage Life Support and the paper notice are exactly "No interval stated".
4. Never put Wellesley's calendar-year expiry on any other company, on Holyoke, or on a blocked card.
5. Never render Unitil's 3 / 6 / 12 box as a protection term. No "protected for 12 months". Show the 220 CMR 25.03 renewal beside it.
6. Never say or imply shutoff protection on an outage-notification card, or outage-list enrollment on a shutoff-protection card. The Eversource letter never says shutoff protection was granted.
7. Never claim higher or faster restoration priority for any program. Do not turn the statute's "fail to restore" into a restoration promise.
8. Never give Holyoke, or any company whose form was not opened, a program, step, renewal or tracker. Name the company from the kit and stop. The kit company beats any utility website dropdown.
9. Never infer a household language. It starts "Not chosen" and changes only when the person changes it. The EJ mean is an unweighted block-group mean with n, not a town-wide rate and not a language.
10. Never set Confirmed. Completing a checklist is not submission; Submitted needs the person's explicit attestation; only the utility confirms.
11. The CPAP sentence appears only on National Grid Outage Life Support, only for CPAP/BiPAP/APAP/VPAP with age over 6 months, verbatim, citing the live form. Never a rejection. Never cite the paper notice for it.
12. Never invent a number, date, URL, quote, address, fax, email, form field or eligibility rule. Not opened = UNVERIFIED on screen. Missing = "not reported", never 0. Quotes are verbatim, including Eversource's repeated "or a chronic serious illness".
13. Never call a facility nearest, safe, accepting, available or a source of backup power, and never sort by distance. Never present a FEMA declaration as a forecast; no county, no FEMA line; a tribal or statewide row is not a county.
14. Never submit to a utility, call a live API, use real household data, give medical advice or decide eligibility.
15. Never add a generic "not a guarantee of power" line; show a no-guarantee sentence only on the record whose source states it.
